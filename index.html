Indigo & Clay

A journey through the ancient kilns, denim streets, and neon dreams of Japan.

The Journey

Your 21-day odyssey begins with Donald's solo exploration of Tokyo's western districts, setting the stage for Ceci's arrival on May 2nd. Together, you will traverse the country—from the sacred peak of Mt. Fuji to the artisan heart of Kyoto, the spiritual deer parks of Nara, and the vibrant streets of Osaka.

This is not just a vacation; it is a pilgrimage to the "Holy Grail" of denim in Kojima and the ancient pottery wheels of Kyoto, ending with a restorative soak in the volcanic waters of Hakone.

History & Soul

Kyoto

For over 1,000 years, the Imperial Capital. It survived WWII bombing to preserve 2,000 temples. It is the spiritual home of the tea ceremony and pottery.

Tokyo (Edo)

Once a small fishing village named Edo, it became the seat of the Shogun in 1603. Today, it is a seamless blend of neon futurism and quiet, hidden shrines.

Hakone

A historic checkpoint on the old Tokaido road connecting Tokyo to Kyoto. Known for its 'onsen' (hot springs) fueled by the active volcano, Owakudani.

The Way of the Traveler

  • 01.

    Silence is Golden

    On trains and buses, silence is respected. Avoid phone calls and keep voices low.

  • 02.

    The Cash Economy

    Many artisan workshops and old temples only accept cash. Carry ¥20,000 (~$130) at all times.

  • 03.

    Tax-Free Shopping

    Carry your passport. Purchases over ¥5,500 are tax-free (10% off). Consumables will be sealed.

Artisan Experiences

Tokyo (Minato)

Uzumako Ceramic Art School

A foreigner-friendly studio specializing in the electric wheel. Create your own tea bowls or sake cups. International shipping available.

Book Session
Okayama (Kojima)

Betty Smith Jeans Museum

The "Holy Grail" of denim. Select raw fabric, choose custom rivets and buttons, and use vintage machines to create your pair.

Reserve Slot
Osaka

Iwasaki Food Models

Craft hyper-realistic 'Sampuru' (plastic food). Use warm wax to shape tempura and lettuce in water. A uniquely Japanese art.

Learn More
Kyoto

Zuikogama Kiyomizu-yaki

Located near the famous Kiyomizu temple. Experience 250 years of tradition using local clay and ash glazes.
